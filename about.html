ABOUT PREMIUM FOOD SUPPLY

Connecting Leading Brands
with Businesses Across Kerala

Premium Food Supply is one of Kerala’s fast-growing food and FMCG distribution companies, built with a clear vision to deliver quality products, reliable service, and strong distribution support to businesses across the state.

From our roots in Kochi, we have grown by building strong relationships with customers, suppliers, and leading brands. Our distribution network is focused on making trusted national and international brands easily accessible to retailers, supermarkets, hotels, restaurants, institutions, and other businesses.
